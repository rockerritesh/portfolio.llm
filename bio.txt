## 🧠 **Professional Summary**

Sumit Yadav is an AI researcher working at the intersection of **mechanistic interpretability, AI safety/alignment, and low-resource NLP**, with 5+ years of experience building production-grade AI systems.

His research studies how safety-aligned LLMs fail silently — through over-refusals, geometric misrepresentation, and surface-level triggers — and how the internal structure of representations can be understood and steered to make models safer and more reliable. This work has been accepted at **ACL 2026 (Main, CORE A\*)**. He also works on bringing language technology to **Maithili** and other under-served languages (creator of **maiBERT**, the first BERT for Maithili).

Alongside research, he designs secure, scalable AI infrastructure: **Zero-Trust** oversight for autonomous agents, **RAG** pipelines, self-hosted LLM serving, and security tooling for the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**. He works on AI safety and agentic systems at **Astha.ai**, writes at sumityadav.com.np, and posts short explainers on YouTube (Audio Obsession).

He is a Computer Engineering graduate of Pulchowk Campus, Tribhuvan University, an active Kaggle competitor, and an open-source contributor.

## 📚 **Publications**

### **SafeConstellations: Mitigating Over-Refusals in LLMs Through Task-Aware Representation Steering** — ACL 2026 (Main Conference, CORE A\*)
- With Utsav Maskey, Mark Dras, Usman Naseem. LLM representations follow distinct per-task "constellation" trajectories in embedding space; SafeConstellations is an inference-time method that selectively steers over-refusal-prone tasks toward non-refusal pathways — **cutting over-refusals by up to 73%** with minimal utility loss and no retraining.
- [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.2056/) · [arXiv:2508.11290](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11290)

### **On the Relationship Between Representation Geometry and Generalization in Deep Neural Networks** — Preprint, 2026 (sole author)
- **Effective dimension**, an unsupervised label-free geometric metric, predicts generalization across vision and language: partial r = 0.75 over 52 ImageNet classifiers (13 architectures), generalizing to NLP encoders and decoder-only LLMs. The link is causal — degrading geometry hurts accuracy while PCA cleanup preserves it.
- [arXiv:2602.00130](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00130)

### **Geometric Phases of Mechanism Formation in Neural Networks** — GLOW 2026 @ IJCAI (Poster; sole author)
- Using linear probes and CKA across dense training checkpoints, shows classification mechanisms form **output-layer-first** within the first ~5% of training (Cohen's d = 3.68). The deep-first pattern holds in from-scratch LLM pretraining (GPT-2 Small, SmolLM2-135M) and reproduces on public Pythia and OLMo-2 checkpoints.
- [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/408634954_Geometric_Phases_of_Mechanism_Formation_in_Neural_Networks)

### **MaiBERT: A Pre-training Corpus and Language Model for Low-Resourced Maithili** — LoResLM @ ACL 2026
- With Raju Kumar Yadav, Utsav Maskey, Gautam Siddharth Kashyap, Ganesh Gautam, Usman Naseem. The first monolingual BERT for Maithili, reaching **87.02% accuracy** on news classification — outperforming NepBERTa and HindiBERT — and open-sourced. (Earlier preprint: "Can maiBERT Speak for Maithili?")
- [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2026.loreslm-1.38/) · [arXiv:2509.15048](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15048) · [Model](https://huggingface.co/rockerritesh/maiBERT_TF)

### **Revolutionizing Currency Security: A YOLOv8-Based Approach for Detecting Counterfeit Nepali Banknotes** — J. Bus. Econ. Stud., 2024
- YOLOv8 for counterfeit Nepali banknote detection; true-positive recall up to 0.9863 on the back face, adaptable across hardware.
- [DOI](https://doi.org/10.61440/JBES.2024.v1.47)

### **Machine Learning Analysis of Tirhuta Lipi** — Technical Report, 2023
- Character recognition for the Tirhuta (Mithilakshar) script — MobileNet embeddings + logistic regression reach **97% accuracy**, enabling OCR for a low-resource script.
- [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/373370042_Machine_Learning_Analysis_of_Tirhuta_Lipi)

### **Support Vectors Are a Better Way of Text Classification for Imbalanced Data** — 2023
- A TF-IDF + n-gram support-vector pipeline that outperforms neural baselines on 100+ imbalanced classes, with incremental retraining.
- [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/371514138_SUPPORT_VECTORS_ARE_A_BETTER_WAY_OF_TEXT_CLASSIFICATION_FOR_IMBALANCED_DATA)
